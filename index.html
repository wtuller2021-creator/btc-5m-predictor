Accuracy tracking is resolution-based. A prediction is not counted as correct or wrong until Polymarket's resolved market data reports the winning side. If the resolution endpoint is temporarily unavailable, the row stays Pending verification and the app retries automatically. This prevents the ledger from guessing an outcome from a live price.

How this version behaves

1. Detects the current 5-minute BTC market.   2. Collects Polymarket's public Chainlink BTC/USD stream during the cycle.   3. At exactly +90 seconds, locks one UP or DOWN decision and never changes it.   4. After the market closes, verifies the actual Polymarket result and updates the ledger.   5. Stores the full record in your browser so your history survives refreshes.

This is a transparent research model, not a guarantee of profit. The ledger is designed to help you measure whether the model actually has an edge before risking money.
